Overview

We develop equipment and components around your process requirements.

FLC.

FLC INC.

Equipment and components,
developed for your process.

Founded in 2020, FLC develops semiconductor and display equipment and components. Our focus includes die bonders, process tools and kits, and core consumables, with equipment development, domestic manufacturing and custom production.

Company history

  1. 2020

    The start of FLC

    Originated in the second SK hynix in-house venture program · Incorporated on July 20 · Venture company certification history

  2. 2021

    Building our engineering foundation

    Investment secured · Supplier registration · Equipment assembly infrastructure established in Cheonan

  3. 2022

    Local component development

    EMI Back Plate localization · Materials, parts and equipment specialist certification and venture certification renewal history

  4. 2023

    Supply and joint development evaluation

    EMI Back Plate supply agreement · Joint development evaluation of a dedicated thin die high-stack system

  5. 2024–2025

    Thin die and HBM components

    Development and supply of Thin Die Ejectors, HBM tools and Saw Kits
