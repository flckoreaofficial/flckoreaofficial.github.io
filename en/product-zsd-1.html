ZSD-1

Stack Die Bonder

A die bonder for precision thin die stacking and automatic tool changes

FLC.
Semiconductor equipmentProduction quality qualification complete

A die bonder for precision thin die stacking and automatic tool changes

Picks individual dies from a wafer and bonds them to a substrate. Dual pickup and dual bond heads support NAND and DRAM stack die bonding processes.
