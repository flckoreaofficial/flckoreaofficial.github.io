RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Custom development & roadmap

We develop structures and specifications for your equipment environment.

FLC.

Custom development process

We start with your equipment environment and target conditions, then refine specifications through design and sample evaluation. Review scope and steps depend on the product.

  1. 01

    Requirements

    Confirm equipment, product dimensions, process issues and target conditions.

  2. 02

    Engineering review

    Assess feasibility and the required development scope.

  3. 03

    Design & samples

    Review structures and materials, then build evaluation samples.

  4. 04

    Evaluate & refine

    Incorporate customer process evaluation results and improvement requests.

  5. 05

    Supply & support

    Supply to agreed specifications and provide follow-up technical support.

HBM thin die pickup

Process need
A die separation structure is needed for the bump surface and pickup conditions.
Engineering approach
Ejector and M-Collet design review
Application & evaluation
Development and production application are documented in the source presentation.

Eject Hood improvement

Process need
Review replacement scope for integrated hoods and component differences between equipment models.
Engineering approach
Detachable hood and common body
Application & evaluation
A structure that reduces replacement parts. The extent of improvement requires evaluation for each equipment configuration.

Wafer Chuck Table

Process need
Review wafer thickness variation and the support structure.
Engineering approach
Chuck structure, bonding method, mounting and water inlet review
Application & evaluation
Test results are included in the source material; TTV is evaluated under actual process conditions.
